Chapter 04 · How they get in

A hacker needs no genius. One mistake of yours is enough

Almost every breach starts with something mundane: an old plugin, a weak password, a forgotten admin panel. This is what they look for first.

Common mistakes

  • 01CMS and plugins go months without updates
  • 02One password everywhere and no second factor
  • 03The admin panel is open to the whole internet
  • 04No backups, or backups nobody has ever tested
  • 05Former staff and contractors still have access
  • 06Passwords and keys sit in code and chat threads
  • 07Nobody reads the logs — a breach is noticed months later
  • 08Security is something “the developer will do when there is time”

Common vulnerabilities · OWASP Top 10
